Frequently asked

Quick answers to the questions we get most. Can't find what you need? Scroll up and send us a message.

Do you deliver? What's the minimum order?

Yes — we deliver within 8 miles of each location. Minimum order is $15 for delivery, and we offer free delivery on orders over $35. Delivery hours run 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Absolutely. The Veggie Delight is fully vegan by default, and we offer marinated tofu as a protein swap on any sandwich. Most breads are vegan-friendly — just ask at the counter.

How far in advance should I book catering?

We recommend 48 hours notice for groups under 50, and at least one week for larger events or custom menus. That said, we'll always do our best to accommodate last-minute requests.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash at the counter. Corporate accounts can be arranged for regular office orders — just get in touch.
