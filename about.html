Our Story

A kitchen built on stubbornness

The first Deli & Sandwich was a 400-square-foot storefront with a secondhand oven, a chalkboard menu, and a line of regulars who showed up before we even had a sign. We had one rule back then, and we still have it today: if it isn't worth slowing down for, it doesn't leave the kitchen.

That meant rethinking every part of the process — sourcing bread from a baker two blocks away, curing our own pastrami, roasting our own peppers, and refusing to serve anything we wouldn't proudly hand to our own families.

The best sandwich is the one you think about the next day.
